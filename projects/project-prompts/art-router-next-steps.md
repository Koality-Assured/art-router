# Art-router next steps

Use this prompt only when a human starts the next phase:

> Continue the art-router harness from the current repository state. First inspect `AGENTS.md`, `routing/`, the current artistic standards, and the upstream `ai-harness-core` comparison notes. Keep normative decisions in `docs/standards/`, exploratory findings in `research/`, operational patterns in `supporting/`, and situational prompts in `projects/project-prompts/`.
>
> Expand the work in bounded passes: (1) research additional artistic representation categories and update the source-backed standards; (2) identify missing technical, accessibility, safety, cultural, authorship, and delivery controls; (3) add supporting reference pages and retrieval terms; (4) create or refine specialist agents and skills only where a repeated responsibility is proven; (5) generate representative assets across 2D, motion, 3D, web, tattoo, photography, and icon/favicon cases; (6) validate each generation against the standards, record failures and measurable quality criteria, and promote durable lessons to the owning source area; (7) update routing, tests, memory, and change-history. Keep the harness generic, preserve upstream engine improvements, use isolated worktrees for mutations, and stop for ambiguity when a decision changes scope or safety.

## Suggested validation set

- One constrained painting or illustration with a declared audience and emotional intent.
- One minimal graphic mark plus favicon/icon exports at small sizes and in monochrome.
- One photograph-like scene with explicit subject, lighting, lens, and ethical constraints.
- One tattoo or body-art design checked for placement, line weight, aging, and informed consent.
- One responsive artistic website or interactive canvas checked for keyboard access, reduced motion, contrast, and fallback behavior.
- One short animation and one CGI scene checked for continuity, timing, camera, lighting, render settings, and delivery metadata.

## Required handoff

Return a compact matrix of generated cases, standards exercised, observed failures, human review decisions, and next changes. Do not claim a standard is validated until at least one real generation or reproducible fixture has exercised it.
